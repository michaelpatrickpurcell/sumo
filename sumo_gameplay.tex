\section*{Gameplay}

A bout plays out over a series of rounds.
Each round, you will simultaneously select move cards, play those cards, and adjust the balance of both wrestlers.

A bout ends when either wrestler loses their balance.
That wrestler loses the bout.
If both wrestlers lose their balance at the same time, then whoever currently has the advantage wins the bout.

\subsection*{Selecting Move Cards}
If your draw pile is empty, collect your pile of previously-played move cards without reordering them, then turn the entire pile face down to form a new draw deck.

Draw cards from your draw pile until you have two move cards in your hand.
Then, choose one move card from your hand and place it face down in front of you.

\subsection*{Playing Move Cards}
When both players are ready, reveal your move cards by turning them face up so that different ends point at each player.

Be careful about how you reveal your move card.
Flipping a move card along its short edge is not equivalent to flipping it along its long edge.

\subsection*{Adjusting the Wrestlers' Balance}
Move your wrestler according to the colored numbers on the ends of \textbf{both} move cards that are pointed at you.

A number's color indicates direction. Move towards the end of the balance card that matches the number's color. 

A number's magnitude indicates distance. Move a number of spaces on the balance card equal to the number's magnitude.
