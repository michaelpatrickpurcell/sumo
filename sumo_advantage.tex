\subsection*{Advantage}
If both wrestlers lose their balance at the same time,
the player with the advantage wins the bout.
The advantage card is used to indicate who has the advantage.

Each player has one move card (the 0/0 card) that has a gold border.
When you play this move card, you may take the advantage card from your opponent. 

If both players play their gold-bordered move cards at the same time, 
then whoever has the advantage card retains it.
