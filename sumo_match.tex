\section*{Match Structure}
After each bout, reset by placing both wrestler cards so that their arrows point at the middle space on the balance card.

Whoever won the previous bout should place their wrestler card gold side up. The other player should place their wrestler card white side up.

Give the advantage card to whoever lost the previous bout.

%Collect the move cards that you played in the previous bout without reordering them, turn the entire pile face down and place it under of your draw deck.

To win the match, you must win two consecutive bouts. That is, you must win while your wrestler card is gold side up.
