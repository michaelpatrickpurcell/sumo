\section*{Prelude}
Before the first bout of the match, you will perform a ring-entering ceremony.
During this ceremony, you will choose the initial ordering of your move cards and show that ordering to your opponent.

Arrange your move cards face down in a single row in front of you.
The leftmost card will be the topmost card of your starting draw deck during the subsequent match while the rightmost card will be the bottommost card of that deck.

Whoever decides upon their ordering first should claim the advantage card and will start the match with the advantage.

When both players have decided on their ordering, turn your cards face up.
Ensure your opponent has time to study your chosen ordering.
Then, gather your cards (without reordering them) and place them face down to form your draw deck.
