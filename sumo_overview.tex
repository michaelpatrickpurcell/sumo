\section*{Overview}
相撲 (SUMO) is an 18-card sumo wrestling game for two players. It can be played in about fifteen minutes.

During the game, you will try to throw your opponent off balance before they can do the same to you.
To do so, you will simultaneously play cards, each of which will affect both wrestlers balance.

You will compete in a match that consists of a series of bouts.
To win the match, you must win two consecutive bouts.
