\section*{Set Up}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item Decide who will play as red and who will play as blue.
\item Give each player the wrestler card and the move cards of their color.
\item Position the balance card such that each player is sitting near the side of their opponent's color. That is, the red player should be seated near the blue side of the balance card and vice versa.
\item Place your wrestler card, white side up, with the arrow pointing at the middle space on the balance card. The two wrestler cards should be placed on opposite sides of the balance card.
\item Place the advantage card to one side.
